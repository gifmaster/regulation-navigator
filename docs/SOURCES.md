# Sources

Rules last reviewed 26 October 2026. Each rule file cites articles; this page lists the texts behind them.

## EU AI Act

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), in force 27 July 2026: high-risk duties for Annex III systems from 2 December 2027, Annex I from 2 August 2028
- Article 73 on serious incidents: [AI Act Service Desk, European Commission](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-73)
- Danish authorities: Digitaliseringsstyrelsen (notifying authority, market surveillance authority, single point of contact), Datatilsynet, Domstolsstyrelsen

## GDPR

- Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Art. 35 on data protection impact assessments
- EDPB/Article 29 Working Party, *Guidelines on DPIA* (WP248 rev.01): nine criteria, two or more usually means a DPIA is needed
- Datatilsynet's list of processing that always requires a DPIA (Art. 35(4)). Not reproduced in the rules; the tool tells users to check it
- Digital Omnibus proposals on GDPR (legitimate interest for AI, breach threshold, 96 hours): proposals only, not applied. [Mayer Brown, January 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/eu-omnibus-proposal-key-changes-for-global-businesses)

## NIS2

- Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Art. 2, 3, 4, 20, 21, 23 and Annexes I and II
- Danish Act No. 434 of 6 May 2025 (general act), in force 1 July 2025
- Danish Act No. 258 of 6 March 2025 (energy sector), in force 7 March 2025
- Danish Act No. 435 of 6 May 2025 (telecom sector), in force 1 July 2025
- Financial digital infrastructure: Financial Business Act chapter 19 c, in force 18 October 2024
- [Lex Mundi: NIS2 implementation in Denmark](https://www.lexmundi.com/guides/status-of-the-nis2-implementation-act-in-the-european-union/jurisdictions/europe/denmark/), on the sector acts and that the size cap does not apply to energy and telecom
- [Bird & Bird: Nordic NIS2 implementation](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/denmark/update-from-the-nordic-countries-on-the-nis2-directive-implementation), on registration (1 October 2025), no administrative fines, and no personal liability for the management body in the Danish act
- [Cyberday: the Danish cybersecurity act](https://www.cyberday.ai/blog/what-is-cybersikkerhedsloven-danish-nis2)

## DORA

- Regulation (EU) 2022/2554: scope in Art. 2(1) and exclusions in Art. 2(3), checked against the [published text](https://www.springlex.eu/en/packages/dora/dora-regulation/article-2/)
- Art. 28 to 30 on ICT third-party risk and key contractual provisions
- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301, Art. 5, time limits for incident reports: [summary with the text](https://advisera.com/cdr-2025-301/time-limits-for-the-initial-notification-and-for-the-intermediate-and-final-reports/)
- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1772 on classification of ICT-related incidents

## Known simplifications

- Group size: users are asked to count the whole group. Partner and linked enterprise rules (Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC) are not modelled in detail.
- DORA Art. 2(4) Member State options and DORA weekend and bank holiday relief are not applied.
- NIS2 designation of small entities (Art. 2(2)(b) to (e)) is mentioned, not assessed.
- AI Act Art. 27: the fundamental rights impact assessment does not apply to high-risk systems for critical infrastructure (Annex III point 2). The tool does not ask which Annex III area applies, so it may suggest a FRIA where the Risk Check would not.
